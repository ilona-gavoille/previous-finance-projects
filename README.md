## 1. Spotify Valuation | DCF Model

### Objective
Estimate the intrinsic value of Spotify using a **Discounted Cash Flow (DCF)** model, and assess whether the stock appears undervalued or overvalued relative to its market price.

### Approach
- Analysis of Spotify's business model, revenue drivers and historical financials
- Forecast of free cash flows over an explicit projection period
- Estimation of the discount rate (WACC) and terminal value
- Derivation of enterprise value, equity value and implied value per share
- Sensitivity analysis on key assumptions

## 2. US Airline Industry | Financial Statement Analysis

### Objective
Compare the financial health and performance of **8 US airline companies** over **5 years** through ratio analysis, then test whether financial performance is reflected in market performance.

### Companies analysed
`[American Airlines]`, `[Delta Air Lines]`, `[United Airlines]`, `[Alaska Air Group]`, `[Southwest Airlines]`, `[Allegiant Air]`, `[JetBlue Airways]`, `[Frontier Airlines]`

### Methodology

**Step 1: Financial statements collection**
Income statements, balance sheets and cash flow statements for each company over a 5-year period (`[2021-2025]`).

**Step 2: Financial ratio computation**
For every company and every year, a wide set of ratios was calculated.

**Step 3: Ranking**
For each ratio and each year, the **2 best** and **2 worst** performing companies were identified.

**Step 4: Market performance projection**
Based on these rankings, groups of best/worst companies were built for each year, and their **cumulative performance** was projected over the following **24 months** using historical market data.

**Step 5: Market comparison**
The resulting performance curves were compared against the **S&P 500** as a benchmark, in order to draw conclusions on whether strong (or weak) fundamentals translate into outperformance (or underperformance) in the market.

### Key questions addressed
- Do companies with the best ratios outperform the market over the next 24 months?
- Do companies with the weakest ratios underperform?
- Which ratios appear to be the most informative for the airline sector?

### Deliverables
- 📄 Report (PDF)
- 📊 Presentation (PPTX)
- 📈 Excel workbook
